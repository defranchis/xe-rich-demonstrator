# xe-rich-demonstrator

Fast optical simulation of the pressurised-xenon RICH pre-demonstrator (ARC / FCC-ee), with β = 1 toy models. The fast simulation is the `xerich` 3D-tracer chain; the toy models are the β = 1 geometric drivers (`cosmic_acc*.py`, `toy_*.py`); the full simulation is ARC's Geant4 framework. Xe is studied as the gas radiator in place of the C₄F₁₀ baseline, which has a high global-warming potential; the reference pressure is Xe 2 bar (`constants.P_REF`), with scans over 1–3 bar. [`docs/design_options.md`](docs/design_options.md) summarises every option explored, including commercial mirrors, SiPM, aerogel and cosmic acceptance.

## Model assumptions and simplifications
- Reference pressure: Xe 2 bar (`constants.P_REF`) for the defaults, the headline numbers and the reference figures; drivers with a single pressure take `--pressure` (default `P_REF`). The regression check against `legacy/` passes 3 bar, the pressure of the legacy scripts.
- Xe refractive index (`media.n_xe`, option `model` / `--xe-index`): by default Börzsönyi et al., Appl. Opt. 47, 4856 (2008) (273 K, taken as 273.15 K, and 1000 mbar); `bm1981` selects Bideau-Mehu et al. (1981) (0 °C, 1 atm). Either is scaled with Lorentz–Lorenz and the virial molar density (B ≈ −130 cm³/mol at 293 K; checks in [`docs/design_options.md`](docs/design_options.md) §1.2).
- Photon yields are emitted Frank–Tamm yields: no photodetector QE, mirror reflectivity or window transmission is applied unless a script states otherwise.
- The β = 1 cosmic toys (`cosmic_acc*.py`, `toy_aerogel.py`, `toy_scan.py`, `toy_prox.py --legacy`) are pure geometry: β = 1 muons with a cos²θ intensity, I0 = 70 m⁻²s⁻¹sr⁻¹, and a mirror clear aperture of 90 % of the diameter. QE, Rayleigh scattering, vessel walls and the SiPM fill factor are not modelled. Aerogel photons leaving through the tile sides are counted as lost.
- Momentum-aware cosmic models (`muons.MuonSpectrum`, `cosmic`, `cosmic_spectrum.py`, `cosmic_rates.py`, `toy_prox.py`): muons from a joint momentum–zenith spectrum at sea level, d²I/dp dΩ of Guan et al., arXiv:1509.06176 (default; modified Gaisser formula with Chirkin's cos θ*), Reyna, hep-ph/0604145 (ζ = p cos θ scaling, fitted above 1 GeV/c) or Shukla–Sankrith, arXiv:1606.06907 (energy-loss form, built from their derivation, Eqs. (8)–(9); `shukla-eq10` is their factorised Eq. (10)), each with its own absolute normalisation (`norm='I0'` rescales to 70 m⁻²s⁻¹sr⁻¹ above 1 GeV/c); 0.2 < p < 1000 GeV/c; azimuth uniform; no altitude correction (`altitude_factor` raises NotImplementedError) and no geomagnetic effect. Each muon radiates at its own β: cos θ = 1/(nβ) and the Frank–Tamm yield ∝ 1 − 1/(n²β²) per wavelength. Overburden (optional, information only) and absorber cuts use the PDG CSDA muon range (`muon_data.RANGE_TABLES`, 10 MeV–100 GeV kinetic energy; a power law through the last two rows above). The 3D tracer treats the tile side faces as black (a photon reaching one ends there, also on its way back from the mirror, as for a black holder frame).
- SiPM: the ARC prototype board, 2" × 2" with four Hamamatsu arrays of 8 × 8 SiPMs at 3.2 mm pitch (S13361-3050NE-08, to be confirmed; [`docs/design_options.md`](docs/design_options.md) §4). The scripts use a continuous square of 50.8 mm (2") or 51.2 mm (the 2 × 2 arrays) with this pitch; `cosmic_rates.py --sensor-model board` uses the board pixel map (footprint 51.6 mm).
- Detector inputs, options of `cosmic_rates.py` (`cli.detector_inputs`; recorded in every output, see Provenance). The defaults are the validated baseline (`cli.BASELINE`, values from the named constants); `--preset published` gives the model of the tables published before it (ARC QE, continuous 2" square, reflectivity 1, constant tile index, porosity 0.97, no board reflection, vertical-muon Rayleigh factor in configuration 4, `--nphot 180`). `--preset baseline-board` is the default and is kept for existing commands; explicit options override a preset.
  - `--response` (default `s13361`): Hamamatsu S13361-3050 PDE (`cherenkov.pde_s13361`; datasheet KAPD1054E07, typical curve at 25 °C and 3 V overvoltage, through the epoxy window), or `arc_qe`, the ARC simulation QE.
  - `--mirror-r` (default `nominal`): `nominal` (assumed curve from the Edmund enhanced-Al spec, not measured; `constants.MIRROR_REFL_NOMINAL`), `1` or a flat value in (0, 1]; the absorbed weight ends as `mirror absorption`.
  - `--sensor-model` (default `board`): the board pixel map (`layout.Board`: four 25.8 mm packages, footprint 51.6 mm, 16 × 16 pads of 3.0 × 3.0 mm at 3.2 mm pitch, active fraction 0.879 inside ±25.6 mm), or `square`. The board replaces the 2" sensor only; the larger layouts (`--groups s61`) stay square. Containment fractions and arcs use the 51.6 mm footprint; photon counts use the active pads (`det_qe`, main tables); the board table's photons on active pads leave out the muon's pad (`det_qe_ring`, configuration 4: `act_ring`). Ring hits: binary pad hits, P = 1 − exp(−μ_pad) per pad (`analysis.pad_hits`), of the ring photons without Rayleigh scatter, leaving out the pad crossed by the muon; no crosstalk, no dark counts. Pad hits are biased low at small `--nphot` (default 7200, `cli.NPHOT_DEFAULT`).
  - `--aerogel-index` (default `single-pole`), `--aerogel-lambda-ref` [nm] (default 400): tile dispersion (`media.aerogel_medium`); the dispersive tile equals the run's tile index, pore shift included, at `--aerogel-lambda-ref`.
  - `--porosity-vac` (default 0.935, `constants.AEROGEL_POROSITY_AIRREF`): porosity of the `pore-vac` tile and of its pore-gas signal; `--pore-t` [K]: temperature of the gas in the pores (default `constants.T_PORE`).
  - `--board-r-spec`, `--board-r-diff` (defaults `constants.BOARD_R_SPEC` 0.11, `BOARD_R_DIFF` 0.10, from Congedo et al., arXiv:2602.20139; values in [0, 1), their sum and the PDE are not checked against 1): specular and diffuse (Lambertian) reflectance of the board, flat in wavelength, used with `--sensor-model board` only. The light reaching the footprint is shared into detected (response), reflected and absorbed, so the ring hits and every other output are unchanged. `cosmic.board_reflections` re-launches the reflected light through the same scene (own random stream, two board reflections, no partial Fresnel reflections of it) in configuration 1 and the configuration-5 gas-ring runs, whose outputs use it; landing on active pads it is other light, never ring hits (`refl_qe`, `pads_refl`). Caveats: flat in λ (the measured reflectance drops below about 380 nm), nothing is reflected beyond the 51.6 mm footprint, black walls, no window slab (refused); not modelled in configuration 4 (no mirror; the light returning to the board is below 0.3 % of the ring photons for vertical muons, `scripts/investigations/board_reflection/config4_size.py`).
  - `--prox-rayleigh` (default `per-photon`): Rayleigh transmission of the configuration-4 aerogel photons along each photon direction from its emission depth to the tile exit, FT × response-weighted over wavelength with the run's tile index (`sim.rayleigh_transmission`), for the photon counts and the pad hits alike (npz key `tile_tr`, the mean transmission of the photons on the sensor; with the board `act` and `act_ring` are transmission-weighted). The spectrum is the β = 1 one for every muon. `vertical` applies the factor of a vertical muon to every muon (`sim.proximity_rayleigh`). Known approximation: the configuration-4 containment and pads use one Cherenkov angle, at n = 1.03, while the yield uses the dispersive index; this underestimates the ≥ 80 % ring rate by up to about 2 %, growing with the gap (+0.56 ± 0.06 / +0.90 ± 0.07 / +1.9 ± 0.4 % at gaps 30 / 50 / 70 mm, Xe 2 bar, plain tile; `scripts/investigations/config4_single_angle/c4_dispersion_selection.py`).
  - Muons are traced in chunks of at most `CHUNK` muons and `CHUNK_PHOTONS` photons (`cosmic_rates.py`), which bounds the memory of a worker; at `--nphot 180` every chunk holds `CHUNK` muons.
  - Ring definitions (full, ≥ 80 %, arcs) are geometric: a ring counts from its light in the ARC QE band whatever the response.

### On-axis hit map (`hitmap_onaxis.py`)
- Layout: mirror R = 406.4 mm, Ø 76.2 mm, clear aperture 90 % of the diameter, reflectivity 1. The 50.8 × 50.8 mm SiPM plane sits at z = f, centred on the axis; below it a 1 mm gas gap, the 50 × 50 × 10 mm aerogel tile, then gas down to the mirror. The whole vessel holds the same gas.
- Media: Xe at 2 and 3 bar (20 °C) from `n_xe` (`--xe-index`); C₄F₁₀ at 1 bar from the ARC simulation table RINDEX__C4F10_PFRICH, held at its 660 nm value above 660 nm (16.6 % of the FT and 6.8 % of the FT×QE photons lie there); aerogel n = 1.03 in every gas, no pore-gas shift. `--aerogel-index` sets the aerogel dispersion: `constant` (default, n = 1.03), `single-pole` (n² − 1 = a₀λ²/(λ² − λ₀²), λ₀ = 83.22 nm) or `k4geo` (the ARC simulation table RINDEX__Aerogel_PFRICH with n − 1 scaled), both equal to 1.03 at `--aerogel-lambda-ref` (default 400 nm).
- Rayleigh scattering in the tile (on by default): mean free path Λ = λ⁴/C with `--rayleigh-c` C = 0.005 µm⁴/cm (the ARC simulation table RAYLEIGH__Aerogel_BelleII; Λ = 1.62 / 5.12 / 25.9 cm at 300 / 400 / 600 nm; 0 switches it off), polarised electric-dipole redirection, weight kept. `--surface-a` A (default 1; ignored when `--rayleigh-c` is 0) gives the transmission T = A exp(−C t/λ⁴) of a straight pass: each face crossing transmits √A and the rest is lost (reason `surface loss`). A path with at least one scatter is class `scattered`; scatters count towards the interaction cap. Applies to gas photons crossing the tile and to aerogel photons.
- The muon's own hit (`--muon-cluster`, default on): the muon crosses the silicon, then a protective window below it (`--window-n` 1.55, `--window-t` 0.1 mm: the S13361 datasheet values, still to be confirmed for the board's arrays). Its Cherenkov light is totally reflected at the window bottom face onto the silicon, in an annulus of radii t·tanθc to 2t·tanθc around the impact point; it is the origin `window`. The direct hit of the muon adds one fired cell (not in the maps). The window acts only on this light; `--window-external` also puts it in the path of all other photons (Fresnel at its bottom face, gas gap reduced by t).
- Photons: β = 1 muon, vertical, on the axis, from the sensor plane to the mirror vertex; emission in the gap, the tile and the gas. Photon energies are sampled from the Frank–Tamm spectrum over 1.3–4.7 eV (264–954 nm), where the ARC SiPM QE is non-zero; the FT×QE results weight each photon by QE(λ), the FT results do not. Each photon uses n(λ) for its emission angle and every refraction.
- Optics: Snell refraction, Fresnel reflection and total internal reflection at both tile faces in both directions, with the Cherenkov linear polarisation carried as a complex field vector through every interface and the mirror (perfect conductor). Rays split their weight into reflected and transmitted branches; paths with more than `--max-reflections` (default 2) partial reflections are dropped and their weight is reported as the interaction cap.
- Not modelled: absorption, Rayleigh scattering in the gas, mirror reflectivity below 1, SiPM reflection (perfect absorber), photon-detection fill factor, angle dependence of the QE and pixelisation. Photons reaching a tile side face are absorbed.

## Layout
| Path | Content |
|---|---|
| `src/xerich/` | the library (below) |
| `studies/` | one driver per study; each writes its files into `--outdir` |
| `tests/` | unit tests and the regression check against `legacy/` |

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| `constants.py` | physical constants, particle masses, cosmic-muon intensity, hardware dimensions, and the fixed values the studies use in place of computed ones |
| `media.py` | Xe n(λ, P, T) (T scalar or array) with the Börzsönyi or Bideau-Mehu formula; C₄F₁₀, air and aerogel indices from the ARC simulation tables; aerogel index with gas-filled pores (`aerogel_index`: porosity 0.97, shift relative to vacuum; `aerogel_index_air`: porosity 0.935, shift relative to air) and aerogel dispersion options; `ConstantIndex` and the `Medium` wrapper (`xenon`, `c4f10`, `air`, `aerogel_constant`, `aerogel_medium`); `Rayleigh` (scattering length λ⁴/C, surface factor) |
| `cherenkov.py` | Cherenkov angle and threshold, Frank–Tamm spectra and integrated yields; FT×QE mean angle and yield vs β (`ftqe_angle_yield`, `theta_xe`), FT×QE rms of the angle (`chromatic_rms`), aerogel-angle shift from Xe in the pores (`pore_signal`, porosity of its vacuum-referenced element as an argument); ARC SiPM QE table (`qe_arc`, `qe_band`), S13361 PDE table (`pde_s13361`) and the response options (`response`); `PhotonSpectrum`: Frank–Tamm photon-energy sampler with optional weight and its yield per mm |
| `layout.py` | dataclasses for the mirror (reflectivity option, `mirror_reflectivity`), sensor (`square` or the `Board` pixel map), aerogel tile, SiPM window and gas radiator; `Phase1Layout` (mirror, gas, tile with optional Rayleigh scattering, gap, optional window, sensor along the axis) and its `scene()` for the tracer |
| `tracer.py` | vectorised 3D ray geometry: Cherenkov cones, Snell refraction with a total-internal-reflection flag, reflection on a sphere, propagation to planes, box intersections; Fresnel s/p coefficients (`fresnel_amplitudes`, `fresnel_powers`), polarised field splitting at a plane interface (`interface_fields`, `interface_split`), Cherenkov and mirror polarisation; polarised Rayleigh redirection (`rayleigh_scatter`); `trace`: weight-splitting tracer through a `Scene` of Fresnel slabs with optional Rayleigh scattering, a spherical mirror and a sensor plane, optionally recording each branch's vertices (`record=True`); a mirror reflectivity R(λ) splits each reflection into a reflected branch and an absorbed one (reason `mirror absorption`, in `REASONS_ALL` after `REASONS`) and adds `wr`, the product of the reflectivities along the branch |
| `muons.py` | cosmic-muon sampling and the muon flux through a horizontal square; surface momentum–zenith spectra (`intensity`, `MuonSpectrum`: tabulated joint sampler, conditional momentum at a given zenith, weights from the cos² law), effective zenith exponent, CSDA range and its inverse |
| `muon_data.py` | PDG muon range tables (concrete, polystyrene, aluminium, iron, lead) and the BESS vertical spectra at Tsukuba and Lynn Lake |
| `cosmic.py` | momentum-aware gas-ring footprint model (`gas_ring_fractions`; `slope_term=True` keeps the slope-term footprint of the legacy β = 1 toys (`sim.footprint_fraction(..., slope_term=True)`)) and batch tracing of many muons at their own β in a `Phase1Layout` (`emit_batch`, `trace_batch`, `fractions`); light reflected by the board (`board_reflections`) |
| `analysis.py` | ring containment on a square sensor, Monte Carlo rates, pixel resolution, binning, Rayleigh-scattered fraction vs wavelength (`scattered_fraction`), binary pad hits and their distribution (`pad_hits`) |
| `sim.py` | setup-level toys: spherical aberration, cosmic ring rates, the 3D mirror toy with aerogel (`MirrorToy`, its gas yield `gas_yield`: `constants.GAS_YIELD_REF` (Börzsönyi index at `P_REF`) scaled by the Frank–Tamm integral of the index model), proximity focusing; Cherenkov photons of one muon in a `Phase1Layout` traced with Fresnel optics (`track_segments`, `emit_photons`, `muon_photons`, `onaxis_muon`); the muon's own hit from its light in the SiPM window (`window_cluster`, `cluster_stats`); `proximity_fractions`: per-muon proximity containment at any aerogel angle (`points=True` adds the sensor-plane points and their mask, `paths=True` the path lengths in the tile); `proximity_photons`, `proximity_rayleigh`, `rayleigh_transmission`: FT × response photons of the proximity aerogel light and their Rayleigh transmission (vertical muon, per path length) |
| `plotting.py`, `sketch.py`, `cli.py` | shared plot style and helpers, the setup sketch, the `--outdir` option and the `--thc-mrad` option (`cli.thc`, `cli.thc_value`: fixed Xe angle in place of the index model) and the `--slope-term` option (`cli.slope_term`: the legacy footprint slope term); the detector-input options, defaults and presets (`cli.detector_inputs`, `cli.inputs`, `cli.BASELINE`, `cli.PRESETS`, `cli.NPHOT_DEFAULT`, `cli.PRESET_NPHOT`, `cli.reflectance`), `--porosity` (`cli.porosity`), `--pressure` (`cli.pressure`) and `--gas-yield` (`cli.gas_yield`); `provenance.py`: the provenance record of the outputs |

## Studies
Run from any directory, e.g. `python studies/toy_aerogel.py --outdir out/`. The default output directory is `$XERICH_OUT` if set, else `xerich-output/` next to the repository checkout. Every driver that uses the Xe index takes `--xe-index {borzsonyi,bm1981}` (default `borzsonyi`); the regression check runs them with `bm1981`, the model of the legacy scripts.

## Tests
`pytest` runs the unit tests (optics, Xe and aerogel index models, Fresnel and polarisation, Rayleigh scattering, tracer closure and weight conservation, the muon's window light; the check of the embedded ARC tables parses `materials_arc_o1_v01.xml` under the k4geo tree root given by `$XERICH_K4GEO` (a key4hep/k4geo checkout or an installed `share/k4geo`) and is skipped when it is unset) and a quick legacy-vs-driver comparison. `XERICH_FULL_REGRESSION=1 pytest` reruns every legacy script and every driver and requires identical printed output and npz arrays (the drivers run with the legacy settings: 3 bar, `--gas-yield 33.5`, the legacy angle; the provenance key is ignored); `python tests/regression.py --help` runs the same steps by hand.

Golden outputs (`tests/test_golden.py`, part of plain `pytest`, about 4 min with 4 workers): small fixed-seed runs of the default drivers and of `--preset published` (`cosmic_rates.py --groups c1 c5 c4` and `cosmic_rates_plots.py`; configuration 1 alone with more muons per task than one chunk holds; the stdout of `raytrace.py`, `cosmic_acc.py` and `toy_prox.py`) must reproduce `tests/golden_reference_<cpu>.json` bit for bit: every npz array (sha256, named by run and field), the summary, the rates and board tables and the stdout, numbers verbatim; a failure names what moved. The reference is per CPU type (`<cpu>` = `avx512`, `avx2`, ... from numpy's CPU features), since float results differ in the last bit between them; on a CPU type without its own reference the digests are skipped (the test says so) and every number of the tables, the stdout and the summary checksums is compared with a relative tolerance of 1e-10. `python tests/golden.py regen` writes the reference of the current CPU type; a regeneration is allowed only together with a change of the input card (design record §4), never to absorb a code change.

Provenance: every npz (key `provenance`), JSON (key `provenance`), markdown table (first line, an HTML comment), text log (first line) and PNG figure (metadata `Description`) a driver writes carries the git commit and a dirty-tree flag (`unknown` and null outside a checkout), the python and numpy versions, the command line, every argument, the resolved detector inputs and the reference constants (`xerich.provenance`). The csv outputs (`hitmap_onaxis_summary.csv`, `prox_onaxis.csv`, `short_f_onaxis.csv`, `muon_window_cluster.csv`) carry none.
